AboutMe.

I'm Sam Sparking, 19, a DJ and producer from St. Gallen. My story started long before I first stood behind a DJ setup.

I grew up on festival videos and found my way into hardstyle early. It was a Defqon.1 video that first showed me what music can really do. The energy, the atmosphere, the connection between DJ and crowd — it hit me instantly and never let go. At 15 the fascination turned into a direction: I watched a DJ hold a club dancefloor completely in his hands. From that moment on, producing and playing wasn't a side project any more — it was the only path that made sense to me.

Four years on, I work on my sound every day, track by track. Inspired by artists like Sound Rush, Sub Zero Project and Headhunterz, I focus on euphoric hardstyle — hard kicks combined with emotional melodies that pull you in and don't let go.

The name Sparking is no accident. It stands for constant energy, a head full of ideas and an optimism that comes through in every production. My sets are built on emotion, storytelling and crowd energy — they create moments where music and audience truly meet.

My mission is simple: make people feel something real. That rush of pure positive energy, that urge to hit repeat straight away, that overwhelming feeling that only happens when the music is exactly right.
