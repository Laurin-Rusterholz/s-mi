AboutMe.

Photo — Sarto Photography

Sam Sparking is a 19-year-old DJ and producer from St. Gallen, Switzerland. His story began long before he ever stood behind a DJ setup.

Growing up watching festival videos and discovering the world of hardstyle at a young age, it was a Defqon.1 video that first showed him what music could really do. The energy, the atmosphere, the connection between DJ and crowd — it hit him immediately and never let go. At 15, seeing a DJ own a dancefloor in a live club setting turned fascination into direction. From that moment, producing and performing wasn't a side project. It was the only path that made sense.

Four years later, Sam is grinding daily, sharpening his sound track by track. Inspired by artists like Sound Rush, Sub Zero Project and Headhunterz, he focuses on euphoric hardstyle — powerful kicks combined with emotional melodies that pull you in and keep you there.

The name Sparking is no coincidence. It stands for constant energy, a head full of ideas and an optimism that bleeds into every production. His sets are built on emotion, storytelling and crowd energy, creating moments where music and audience truly connect.

His mission is simple: make people feel something real. That rush of pure positive energy, that urge to hit repeat, that overwhelming feeling you only get when the music is exactly right.
